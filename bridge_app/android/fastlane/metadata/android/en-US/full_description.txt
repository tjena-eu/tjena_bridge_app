<b>Your own WhatsApp bridge, at home</b>

tjena Bridge runs the open-source mautrix-whatsapp bridge on a spare Android phone that stays at home. Your WhatsApp messages are decrypted only on that phone and on your tjena!chat devices; the tjena server only passes encrypted data along.

<b>Features</b>

• Link one or more WhatsApp accounts with a pairing code
• Copies your recent chat history into tjena!chat
• WhatsApp voice and video calls from tjena!chat
• Encrypted backup of the bridge, to move to a new phone
• Diagnostics: history import, encryption keys, chat names

<b>You need</b>

A spare Android phone (Android 10 or newer, 64-bit) on Wi-Fi and a charger, and a tjena!chat account.

WhatsApp doesn't officially allow third-party clients. The bridge connects as a linked device; use it at your own risk.

Source code: https://github.com/tjena-eu/tjena_bridge
